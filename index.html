University project · Team lead2026

PulseHR

An HR system for companies in Bangladesh that runs everyday HR work and warns HR early when an employee may be about to leave.

  • Everyday HR: attendance with shifts (night shifts too), leave, payroll under the Bangladesh Labour Act with PDF payslips, OKRs, recruitment and a noticeboard
  • Three roles: HR runs the company; a manager approves leave and attendance fixes only for their own department; an employee checks in, applies for leave and downloads payslips
  • Early warning: an attrition-risk score only HR can see, with the factors behind it, and never used to fire someone or set their pay
  • No email needed: password recovery by employee ID, the last 4 digits of the National ID, then an SMS code
  • Tested: 130 unit tests and 165 regression checks run on every change, on SQLite and PostgreSQL

My role: team lead and backend architect in a team of five at Southeast University.
